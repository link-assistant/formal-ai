## How to make pancakes

1. 2 cups (18oz/510g) self-rising or all-purpose flour (See tips below) — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
2. 2 tablespoons of butter or vegetable oil — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
3. 1/2 teaspoon of vanilla extract (optional) — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
4. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat until creamy. Add in the dry ingredients (excluding the salt and baking soda if using self-rising flour). Do not stir the mixture at this point! — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
5. Melt the butter in a microwave-safe bowl. Make sure that it's completely melted; about a minute is sufficient. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
6. Add the butter and milk to the mix. Stir gently, leaving some small clumps of dry ingredients in the batter. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
7. Overmixing your batter is going to develop strong gluten strains, which will make your pancakes very chewy. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
8. Heat the frying pan to a medium-low flame. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
9. Sprinkle a few flecks of water onto your pan. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
10. Pour about 3 tablespoons to 1/4 cup batter from the tip of a large spoon or from a pitcher onto the hot griddle or greased frying pan. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
11. Cook for about two minutes or until the pancake is golden. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
12. Cook the other side until golden and remove. — wikiHow (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, sha256 1cbdf5f7a9d6)
