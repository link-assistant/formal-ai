## Evidence and fair measurements

Every attempt joins session/task/cohort/oracle/engine/tool/source/commit identities; initial memory/cache state; actual messages, thinking and tool effects; immutable pre/post blobs; termination reason; original oracle result; optional child composition; actual external source provenance; replay/verifier results; resource and token observations; and assistance classification. Logs and chain records bind one another. Integrity, authenticated execution, autonomous reasoning, useful delivered changes and published runtime are separate claim levels.

Report success count over all started eligible tasks as a conservative rate, with failures, Unknown and incomplete counts visible and never in the numerator. Invalid tasks/exclusions need predeclared independent reasons and remain listed. Report cohort size and uncertainty; a handful of easy or self-chosen passes cannot establish a broad60–80% claim. Compare identical scopes/budgets and original oracles before/after. Actual payload/token/cost counters are measured; estimates disclose tokenizer and estimator, and unavailable billing is Unknown. Keep coordinator-provided tokens/recipes and token costs separate from Formal AI output. Count useful net changes after review, with generated/vendor/docs/tests classified separately and no padding rewarded.

Empty-cache operational proof uses the real approved trusted services under finite network/time/byte budgets, preserved responses and source digests, integrity/expiry/poisoning controls, no hidden answer cache and no unsafe credentials. Warm-cache tests prove safe reuse and reduced work; offline refusal is its own outcome. The minimal installation manifest must identify every bootstrap item and why it is necessary. Do not ship an unbounded solved-task corpus.
