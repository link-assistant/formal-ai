# Know just enough to get to know anything

Source: the architect's words dated 2026-10-15, preserved verbatim from [issue #1212](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/issues/1212). Spelling and punctuation below are unchanged.

> Also check that we set motto/slogan in formal AI as: `Don't know everything, know just enough to get to know anything`, which means, core of our meta algorithm should be ability to get and formalize just enough data from the internet to solve any task, with priority on coding, as it allows to make self conding (if user gives such permission), so our meta algorithm is trully able to evolve via recursive self improvement, and we don't treat bugs or failuress as something wrong or fatal, we treat it as useful data, evidence that we tried something and it didn't work, so we can try something else (not by searching through all possible solutions, but by finding the most likely, the most simple and logical even better - provable solution).

This note records design direction. Source metadata checks do not establish released-package or repository-Settings acceptance.
