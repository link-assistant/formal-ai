## Issue #1172 Factual QA Subject-Verified Lookup

Issue [#1172](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/issues/1172) (E137,
part of the #1183 umbrella) reports that "What is the capital of Australia?"
was answered with "The capital of the United States is Washington, D.C." —
the alias "us" matched as a raw substring inside "a**us**tralia". A wrong
answer is worse than no answer. This shard pins the delivered word-boundary
fix (R1, R4, R10, R11 of the issue), the subject Q-id gate (R2), seeded
comparisons (R6), questions over prompt-supplied text (R7) and the shared
parity case (R1172-12), and records the open follow-ups (R3, R5, R8, R9 and
the live-data halves of R6/R7) honestly. The root-cause
analysis, the corrected issue-0127 rows, and
the evidence live in `docs/case-studies/issue-1172/`.

| R1172-1 | A subject alias or question keyword may match the normalized prompt only at word boundaries (tokenized comparison), never as a raw substring inside a longer word: "us" must not match inside "australia"; multi-word aliases ("united states") match as consecutive token sequences; CJK surfaces, which have no inter-word spaces, keep substring matching. | Implemented: `FactRecord::matches_normalized` (`rust/src/seed/facts.rs`) now gates every alias and keyword through the new associated `FactRecord::contains_word_sequence`, which tokenizes the phrase with the same `engine::normalize_prompt` the caller applied to the prompt and requires a consecutive token run — the same contract as `seed::meanings::surface_present` (issue #386), mirrored for the fact store's own matching path.
