## Issue #1172 Factual QA Subject-Verified Lookup

Issue [#1172](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/issues/1172) (E137,
part of the #1183 umbrella) reports that "What is the capital of Australia?"
was answered with "The capital of the United States is Washington, D.C." —
the alias "us" matched as a raw substring inside "a**us**tralia". A wrong
answer is worse than no answer. This shard records the original word-boundary
fix, the publisher subject and property checks, the comparison and
passage-only routes, and their cross-runtime replays. The original analysis
is in `docs/case-studies/issue-1172/`. Issue #1613 records the removal of the
bundled publisher captures and the remaining audit of other seed answers in
`docs/case-studies/issue-1613/`.

| ID | Requirement | Status / Evidence |

| R1172-1 | A subject alias or question keyword may match the normalized prompt only at word boundaries (tokenized comparison), never as a raw substring inside a longer word: "us" must not match inside "australia"; multi-word aliases ("united states") match as consecutive token sequences; CJK surfaces, which have no inter-word spaces, keep substring matching. | Implemented: `FactRecord::matches_normalized` (`rust/src/seed/facts.rs`) now gates every alias and keyword through the new associated `FactRecord::contains_word_sequence`, which tokenizes the phrase with the same `engine::normalize_prompt` the caller applied to the prompt and requires a consecutive token run — the same contract as `seed::meanings::surface_present` (issue #386), mirrored for the fact store's own matching path. Pinned by `rust/tests/unit/web-engine-core/issue_1172_factual_qa_subject_match.rs`. |
| R1172-2 | A fact record may answer only when the formalized question's subject (Wikidata Q-id) equals the record's `subject_qid`; alias/keyword matching is retired as the primary mechanism. | Implemented. The fact lookup binds the property to the question, checks whole-word aliases, and verifies the exact Wikidata subject and ranked claim before using its value. The historical subject-gate replays explicitly load test data. |
| R1172-3 | When no seeded fact's subject matches, the engine queries Wikidata live for the requested property and answers from the returned statement with its reference URL. | Implemented. The browser and native solvers derive the subject and property from the request, verify candidate labels or aliases and ranked Wikidata claims, and retain the source URL and content hash. An empty offline cache cannot answer the question. |
| R1172-4 | `fact_store_resolves` returns `false` for a prompt whose subject has no seeded record, so the open-web/live-query planner (issue #989) is not blocked by a false hit. | Implemented: `fact_store_resolves` (`rust/src/solver_handlers/benchmark_prompts.rs`) delegates to the corrected matcher, so "What is the capital of Australia?" resolves to `false` and the planner may release the prompt to the open web instead of discarding the wrong cached answer (issue #1138's regression is not reintroduced: seeded subjects still resolve `true`). | R1172-5 | Definitions ("What does X mean?") resolve via Wiktionary or Wikidata lexemes, never the canned web-search paragraph. | Implemented 2026-10-07: a definition request is answered from the registry's dictionary source before any encyclopedia or search lane, in both runtimes. The word is read through seeded frames — the `word_definition` rows of `data/seed/prompt-patterns.lino` (`what does {term} mean`, `define {term}`, `что означает {term}`, `{term} का क्या अर्थ है`, `{term}是什么意思`, `qué significa {term}`, …) — and looked up in the sources whose registry kind the same rows name (`dictionary`: Wiktionary, through the Free Dictionary entry API for English and each edition's MediaWiki extracts otherwise). Each edition's definition-section titles (`значение`, `adjetivo`/`sustantivo`/…, `शब्दसागर`), example marker (`◆`), annotation labels and inflection marker are seed rows read by one reader, so a new edition is a data change. The answer (localized `word_definition_answer`/`word_definition_sense` templates in `data/seed/multilingual-responses-concept-lookup.lino`) numbers up to three senses with their part of speech and cites the captured URL, its SHA-256 and the license. A seeded concept record still answers first; a word no dictionary capture defines falls through to the next row, which records every consulted source (#1173), so no canned paragraph is ever the answer. Native: `rust/src/solver_handlers/word_definition.rs`, the `concept_lookup` row's contextual step in `rust/src/solver_dispatch.rs` (capture cache, network only with `FORMAL_AI_LIVE_FETCH`); browser: `tryWordDefinition` in `js/worker/formal_ai_worker_concept_lookup.js`, called from `solve` before the Wikipedia lane. Pinned over committed captures (`rust/tests/fixtures/issue-1172-definitions/source-cache`, fetched 2026-10-07: en ephemeral, ru эфемерный, hi क्षणिक, es efímero, zh 苹果 with no Chinese sense) by `rust/tests/unit/issue_1172_word_definition.rs` and `rust/tests/web/issue-1172-word-definition.test.mjs`. Wikidata lexemes are not consulted: the registry's `lexeme_api` returns no senses for the measured surfaces (its own note), so Wiktionary is the dictionary that answers. |

| R1172-7 | Questions over prompt-supplied text are answered purely from the formalized text's own statements, with zero network calls. | Implemented (JavaScript verified locally; Rust twin drafted, awaiting CI): a question over a quoted, prompt-supplied passage (cue `prompt_text_question_cue` in en/ru/hi/zh/es, or an uncued passage of six or more words that opens the prompt and is followed by a question) is answered from the passage's own sentences — the sentence whose words cover at least half of the question's content words (the seed's `statement_function_word`, `interrogative_opener` and cue words removed; CJK compares character pairs) is quoted via `prompt_text_answer`, and `prompt_text_gap` says honestly that the text does not say, naming the uncovered words. No source is consulted and the browser test asserts zero network calls. `tryPromptTextQuestion` (synchronous handler right after `trySummarizationText`, so it precedes the calendar route that used to claim the issue's meeting example) and `try_prompt_text_question` (first step of the native `fact_lookup` row). Statement projection (2026-10-07; JavaScript verified locally, Rust twin drafted and awaiting CI): the covering sentence is projected into subject/predicate/object/time/place slots — a seeded `statement_time_preposition` or `statement_place_preposition` surface (data/seed/meanings-facts.lino, en/ru/es) opens an adjunct phrase that a comma closes; a phrase naming a `calendar_weekday`, `calendar_month_name`, `calendar_hour_reference` or `statement_clock_suffix` surface (stem-compared) or a clock shape is the time, any other place phrase the place, the rest object; the clause before the adjuncts splits into subject (leading function words + first content word) and predicate. The answer states the slots the question asks first (`statement_time_question`/`statement_place_question`/`statement_subject_question` in en/ru/hi/zh/es, via `prompt_text_slot_answer`) when each carries a content word the question does not already say, then the quote, then the whole statement (`prompt_text_statement` and the five `prompt_text_slot_*` templates of data/seed/multilingual-responses-entities.lino); the log carries `prompt_text:statement` (`subject=…;predicate=…;time=…;place=…`) and `prompt_text:asked`. The issue example now answers "From the text — time «from Tuesday to Thursday at 3 pm»; place «in room 204»." `project_statement`/`statement_answer` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/prompt_text_question.rs`, `promptTextStatement`/`promptTextStatementAnswer` in `js/worker/formal_ai_worker_factual_qa.js`; pinned by the same strings in the two R7 projection tests of `rust/tests/web/issue-1172-factual-qa-subject-gate.test.mjs` and in `the_covering_sentence_projects_into_statement_slots`, `an_asked_slot_the_question_already_says_is_not_claimed` and `a_sentence_without_a_seeded_adjunct_keeps_the_quote_alone` (rust/tests/unit/web-engine-core/issue_1172_factual_qa_gate.rs). Open: Hindi and Chinese sentences keep the quote alone: the three phrase meanings now carry hi and zh surfaces (all five languages, so the language-parity ratchet holds at 910), but a sentence in a `verb_final` language of `data/seed/formal-targets.lino` marks time and place with postpositions, which the phrase reader (a preposition opens the phrase) does not read, so both runtimes decline it; CJK has no word boundaries, the subject/predicate split is positional (no part-of-speech tagging, so a multi-word noun before the verb is split wrongly), and an adjunct-free sentence is not projected. |
