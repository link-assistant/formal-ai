## Issue #1176 Quantities, Dates and Statistics Computed Exactly

Issue [#1176](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/issues/1176) (E141,
part of the #1183 umbrella) replaces four classes of canned or wrong answers —
calendar offsets, statistics over stated lists, unit conversion, and
price×count word problems — with exact local computation that shows its
arithmetic. The probes, the before/after evidence, and the honest boundaries
live in `docs/case-studies/issue-1176/`.

| ID | Requirement | Status / Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1176-1 | Quantities are formalized with value and unit, the unit resolved to a Wikidata item with its conversion factor, and conversions are exact decimal arithmetic; "26.2 miles in km" → 42.1648128 km with the factor 1.609344 cited. | Partial: `rust/src/solver_handlers/unit_conversion.rs` computes conversions on an exact `mantissa × 10⁻ˢᶜᵃˡᵉ` decimal (26.2 × 1.609344 = 42.1648128, 10 km → ≈6.2137119 miles, °C→°F as the formula 25 × 9/5 + 32 = 77) and the answer cites the factor it used. Factors, formulas and unit surfaces are seed data in `data/seed/meanings-units.lino`, each unit grounded in its Wikidata item (mile Q253276, inch Q218593, …) — a hand-grounded starter table, not live P2370 reads; units are resolved by lexicon surface, not by full item resolution. Unknown units (furlongs) and ambiguous lists ("5 feet 9 inches in cm") are declined. |
| R1176-2 | Word problems are formalized into quantities and relations (price × count, payment − cost) and solved, with the derivation shown (pens: 20 − 4 × 3 = 8). | Partial: `handle_word_problem` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/statistics.rs` answers the unit-price pattern — "at N dollars each" plus a count (and a payment for the change variant) — as exact decimal arithmetic with the derivation in the answer body (change: 20 − 4 × 3 = 8; total: 4 × 3 = 12), the markers being seed vocabulary in every supported language. Only this pattern is recognised; general relation extraction through the equation solver is not delivered. |
| R1176-3 | Descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation, percentile, range) are meanings with formulas in `data/seed/meanings-statistics.lino`, applied to the extracted list; mean(4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42) = 18, median = 15.5. | Partial: all six seed-defined operations except percentile are implemented in `rust/src/solver_handlers/statistics.rs` — each a meaning with formula and source URL in `data/seed/meanings-statistics.lino`, labels localised from the seed lexemes. Mean 108 / 6 = 18 and median (15 + 16) / 2 = 15.5 are exact; the variance is the exact fraction 5460/36 rendered to seven digits with an explicit ≈ marker (the standard deviation is the one marked approximation). Percentile is not delivered. |
| R1176-4 | Calendar arithmetic reads the formalized offset (quantity and unit: days, weeks, months) and computes modulo the cycle: 100 days after Monday = 14 weeks + 2 days → Wednesday. | Partial: `try_calendar_reasoning` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/calendar.rs` now reads the stated offset — numeral plus unit surface from the seed (`calendar_day`, `calendar_week`), spelled cardinals included, CJK-unspaced "100天" included — and shifts by it: 100 days after Monday is Wednesday with the derivation "100 days = 14 weeks + 2 days"; whole weeks state that the weekday is unchanged. A bare "the day after X" keeps its original ±1 reading. Month offsets and real-calendar date arithmetic (month lengths, leap years) are not delivered; weekday-cycle arithmetic only. |
| R1176-5 | Each answer carries its derivation and all three roots answer identically. | Implemented 2026-10-07: every answer body shows the arithmetic it performed (derivation strings in `data/seed/multilingual-responses-quantities.lino`, 12 intents × 5 languages), the evidence log carries `calendar:offset`, `statistics:<op>`, `unit_conversion:<step>`, `word_problem:<role>` events, and #1184's derivation record (`derivation::finalize_answer`) completes every route. The browser worker and the TypeScript twin answer the same probes (R1176-6). |
| R1176-6 | Worker parity: the unit-conversion, statistics, word-problem and calendar-offset computations answer identically in the browser worker and the TypeScript root (pull-request follow-up row: the three-roots half of R1176-5). | Implemented 2026-10-07: unit conversion, statistics, word problems and calendar offsets have browser-worker twins that reproduce the Rust handlers' exact-decimal arithmetic and derivation text, and `ts/` is regenerated from them; pinned by `rust/tests/web/issue-1176-unit-conversion-parity.test.mjs`, `issue-1176-statistics-parity.test.mjs` and `issue-1176-calendar-offset-parity.test.mjs`. |
