## Issue #1176 Quantities, Dates and Statistics Computed Exactly

Issue [#1176](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/issues/1176) (E141,
part of the #1183 umbrella) replaces four classes of canned or wrong answers —
calendar offsets, statistics over stated lists, unit conversion, and
price×count word problems — with exact local computation that shows its
arithmetic. The probes, the before/after evidence, and the honest boundaries
live in `docs/case-studies/issue-1176/`.

| ID | Requirement | Status / Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1176-1 | Quantities are formalized with value and unit, the unit resolved to a Wikidata item with its conversion factor, and conversions are exact decimal arithmetic; "26.2 miles in km" → 42.1648128 km with the factor 1.609344 cited. | Partial: `rust/src/solver_handlers/unit_conversion.rs` computes conversions on an exact `mantissa × 10⁻ˢᶜᵃˡᵉ` decimal (26.2 × 1.609344 = 42.1648128, 10 km → ≈6.2137119 miles, °C→°F as the formula 25 × 9/5 + 32 = 77) and the answer cites the factor it used. Factors, formulas and unit surfaces are seed data in `data/seed/meanings-units.lino`, each unit grounded in its Wikidata item (mile Q253276, inch Q218593, …) — and the factors are checked against Wikidata itself: `data/cache/wikidata/unit-conversion/P2370.lino` caches each grounded unit's P2370 (conversion to SI unit) statement, fetched 2026-10-07, and `si_factors_match_the_cached_wikidata_conversion_to_si` in `rust/tests/unit/issue_1176_quantities_dates.rs` requires every `si_factor` of `data/seed/si-unit-dimensions.lino` to equal it (14 length, mass and time units). The cache is refreshed by hand, not read live at answer time; units are resolved by lexicon surface, not by full item resolution. Unknown units (furlongs) and ambiguous lists ("5 feet 9 inches in cm") are declined. |
| R1176-2 | Word problems are formalized into quantities and relations (price × count, payment − cost) and solved, with the derivation shown (pens: 20 − 4 × 3 = 8). | Implemented: the unit-price pattern (`handle_word_problem` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/statistics.rs`: change 20 − 4 × 3 = 8, total 4 × 3 = 12) is joined by a general relation reading, `statistics/word_relations.rs` (twin `js/worker/formal_ai_worker_word_relations.js`), that folds the stated numbers by the seeded relation words of `data/seed/meanings-statistics.lino` (`word_problem_gain`, `word_problem_loss`, `word_problem_group`, `word_problem_share`, with a `word_problem_question` cue, in en/ru/hi/zh/es): 5 + 3 = 8, 20 - 7 = 13, 6 × 4 = 24, 24 ÷ 6 = 4, 12 + 5 - 4 = 13, and declines numbers no relation word joins. Pinned by `relation_word_problems_derive_from_the_seeded_relation_words` and `relation_word_problems_decline_numbers_no_relation_joins` in `rust/tests/unit/issue_1176_quantities_dates.rs` and by `rust/tests/web/issue-1176-word-relations.test.mjs`. Relations beyond these four (rates, comparisons such as "3 more than", multi-step fractions) are not read. |
| R1176-3 | Descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation, percentile, range) are meanings with formulas in `data/seed/meanings-statistics.lino`, applied to the extracted list; mean(4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42) = 18, median = 15.5. | Implemented 2026-10-07: all seven seed-defined operations are delivered in `rust/src/solver_handlers/statistics.rs` and its browser twin `js/worker/formal_ai_worker_statistics.js` — each a meaning with formula and source URL in `data/seed/meanings-statistics.lino`, labels localised from the seed lexemes. Mean 108 / 6 = 18 and median (15 + 16) / 2 = 15.5 are exact; the variance is the exact fraction 5460/36 rendered to seven digits with an explicit ≈ marker (the standard deviation is the one marked approximation). The percentile (`statistics_percentile`) is linear interpolation between closest ranks (the C = 1 variant, Hyndman–Fan definition 7, cited to Wikipedia): the rank stated next to the percentile word ("the 90th percentile", "90-й перцентиль", "percentil 90", "第90百分位数") leaves the dataset as a parameter, and the answer is exact with its derivation from the seed template `statistics_percentile_derivation` in five languages — 90th percentile of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5: rank = 1 + (5 - 1) × 90 / 100 = 4.6; 4 + 0.6 × (5 - 4) = 4.6. A missing or out-of-range rank is declined. |
| R1176-4 | Calendar arithmetic reads the formalized offset (quantity and unit: days, weeks, months) and computes modulo the cycle: 100 days after Monday = 14 weeks + 2 days → Wednesday. | Implemented 2026-10-07: `try_calendar_reasoning` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/calendar.rs` (twin: `js/worker/formal_ai_worker_calendar_offset.js`) reads the stated offset — numeral plus unit surface from the seed (`calendar_day`, `calendar_week`, `calendar_month`), spelled cardinals included, CJK-unspaced "100天" and "2个月" included — and shifts by it: 100 days after Monday is Wednesday with the derivation "100 days = 14 weeks + 2 days"; whole weeks state that the weekday is unchanged. Month offsets (`rust/src/solver_handlers/calendar/month.rs`): from a month name (the twelve `calendar_month_name` meanings, Wikidata Q108–Q126) they are exact modulo-12 arithmetic with the derivation shown — 5 months after November: 11 + 5 = 16 ≡ 4 (mod 12), April; from a weekday they have no single answer, because a month is no fixed number of days, so the answer is a clarification that names why and shows the day span the months can cover over a full 400-year Gregorian cycle (3 months span 89 to 92 days) and asks for the starting date. A bare "the day after X" keeps its original ±1 reading. Real-calendar date arithmetic (a dated start plus months, end-of-month clamping) is not part of this row. |
