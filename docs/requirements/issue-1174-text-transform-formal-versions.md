## Issue #1174 Text Transforms Answered Formally

Issue [#1174](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/issues/1174) (E139,
part of the #1183 umbrella) gives the solver the text-transform family —
free-text summarization, register rewriting, grammar correction, genre
writing (Conventional Commits and business email), and sentence-level
translation — as computations over text the request itself carries, with all
recognition vocabulary and response prose in seed data and every honest
boundary (nothing to change, slot missing, word unknown) reported through a
seeded template instead of a guess. The probes, the before/after evidence,
and the honest boundaries live in `docs/case-studies/issue-1174/`.

| ID | Requirement | Status / Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1174-1 | A summarization request that carries its own paragraph ("Summarize this paragraph: …") is answered with a weight-ranked selection of its statements under a ~30% bound (at least two, always at least one short of the input), never an echo of the source; a request that names no text is declined so the seeded-topic handlers keep it. | Delivered: `handle_summarization_request` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/summarization_request.rs` recognizes the act through the `text_summarization_action` role (surfaces in `data/seed/meanings-summarization.lino`, five languages), extracts the payload with `free_text_payload`, and runs the shared formalize → summarize → deformalize pipeline (`crate::summarization`); statements under two are declined, and the selection bound can never equal the input length. |
| R1174-2 | A register-rewriting request ("Rewrite this formally: can u send me the report asap thx") substitutes every seeded informal token with its formal equivalent (casing kept), splices seeded politeness phrase rules, logs each substitution, and returns a body with no informal token; an already-formal text passes through unchanged with an honest note. | Delivered: `handle_rewrite_register` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/text_rewrite.rs` reads word pairs and phrase rules from `data/seed/register-lexicon.lino` (English and Russian), keeps source casing via `rebuild_core`, and answers through the `text_transform_register` response family; the no-change branch renders `text_transform_rewrite_unchanged`. |
| R1174-3 | A grammar-correction request ("Correct the grammar: She don't like apples and he have two cat.") re-agrees adjacent third-person subject + verb pairs and pluralizes a numeral's noun (irregular plurals and uncountables respected), logging each fix with the rule that fired; a clean text is reported as already grammatical. | Delivered: `handle_grammar_correction` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/text_rewrite.rs` applies the `subject_verb_rule`, `numeral_noun_rule`, `plural_exception` and `uncountable_noun` records of `data/seed/agreement-rules.lino` token by token; corrections render line by line through `text_transform_grammar_line` with the rule name, the body through `text_transform_grammar_result`, and the clean case through `text_transform_grammar_clean`. |
| R1174-4 | A commit-message request ("Write a commit message: correct an off-by-one error in the pagination helper") composes a Conventional Commits line from the described change — verb mapped to the seeded type, scope after the seeded preposition, effect between — and names the genre's missing slots through the incomplete template instead of fabricating them. | Delivered: the `commit_message` arm of `handle_genre_writing` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/text_rewrite.rs` fills the `{type}({component}): {imperative} {effect}` and no-scope templates of `data/seed/writing-genre-styleguides.lino` (styleguide URL logged); a request missing the verb or effect answers through `text_transform_genre_incomplete` at reduced confidence. |
| R1174-5 | An email request ("Write an email to my team: I am taking a day off on Friday because I am tired.") composes the seeded part frames — greeting with recipient, intent, reason after the seeded cue, weekday date, closing — in the styleguide's declared order. | Delivered: the `email` arm of `handle_genre_writing` reads the part sequence, frames, recipient preposition, possessives and reason cue from `data/seed/writing-genre-styleguides.lino`, resolves the weekday through the `calendar_weekday` role, and renders optional parts (reason, date) only when their cues are present. |
| R1174-6 | A translation request whose text has no quotes or backticks ("Translate to Russian: The weather is nice today, let's go for a walk.") still translates: the free sentence after the command head goes word by word through the pipeline, function words are dropped and logged, resolved words carry their meaning ids, unknown words are named through the seeded template rather than hidden, and a sentence with nothing translatable reports the honest gap. | Delivered: `translate_free_sentence` in `rust/src/solver_handlers/mod.rs` (reached from `try_translation` when no quotable surface exists and source ≠ target) calls `TranslationPipeline::translate_sentence` (`rust/src/translation/pipeline.rs`), which resolves each token against seeded compositional lemmas and the cache before any network request, keeps the source token order, and preserves terminal punctuation; unknown words append the `text_transform_translation_unknown` note. |
| R1174-7 | The whole family is a pure function of seed data — cues, pairs, rules, frames, and response prose live in `data/seed/` with five-language response parity — every new token the seed files introduce resolves under the total-closure audit, and no hardcoded-language or literal-predicate debt is added. | Delivered: recognition vocabulary in `register-lexicon.lino`, `agreement-rules.lino`, `writing-genre-styleguides.lino`, `meanings-summarization.lino`; responses in `multilingual-responses-text-transform.lino` (en/ru/hi/zh/es); the new intent keys and identifiers grounded by five-field definitions in `meanings-text-transform.lino` (registered in `data/meta/seed-registry.lino`); all six files measured net-zero unresolved under `scripts/audit-total-closure.py`, and the literal-predicate count stays at its 536 ceiling. |
| R1174-8 | The family is covered by automated tests pinning each probe's intent and computed body, including the honest boundaries (no text to summarize, already formal, already grammatical, missing slots, declined prompts). | Delivered: `rust/tests/unit/issue_1174_text_transform.rs` exercises the handlers directly (the dispatch wiring belongs to the #1175 routing work delivered in the same pull request); its registration in `rust/tests/unit/mod.rs` and the dispatch entries are owned by the coordinating session. |
| R1174-9 | Every answer for these five classes carries its derivation as links (formalized statements, the source-language surface, the target grammar rule or style-guide clause applied) inspectable via `formal-ai explain <answer>`, per the umbrella's white-box requirement (#1183 acceptance item 2). | Implemented 2026-10-07: the handlers log their derivations as evidence-log events (`register_rewrite` per substitution, `grammar_correction` per fix with its rule, `genre_styleguide`/`genre_composition`, `summarization_statement`/`_bound`/`_selected`, and the `translation_*` steps), and the #1184 derivation record now carries them as its `rule` stage: `data/seed/derivation-schema.lino` declares each of those kinds with `collects rule`, `Derivation::record_for` (`rust/src/derivation.rs`) projects every declared kind into an `AppliedRule` (kind + detail), the record round-trips through Links Notation, and `formal-ai explain <answer-id>` prints them under `stage applied_rules` ("not recorded" when a route applied none). A handler joins by naming its event kind in the schema, not by a Rust edit. Pinned by `applied_rules_are_projected_round_tripped_and_explained` and `a_grammar_correction_answer_carries_its_rules_in_the_derivation` in `rust/tests/unit/issue_1184_derivation_records.rs`. Since 2026-10-07 the browser worker projects the same `rule` stage for its answers (R1184-9, `js/worker/formal_ai_worker_derivation.js`, pinned by `rust/tests/web/issue-1184-derivation-worker.test.mjs`). |
