## Issue #1186 Formalization as a User-Facing Task

Issue [#1186](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/issues/1186) (E150,
part of the E127 umbrella #1183): "Formalize in first-order logic: Every
student who studies passes the exam." answered with the canned web-search
paragraph on 0.347.0. The issue asks for a formalization handler that parses
a quantified sentence into a clause, renders it into first-order logic, Lean
and Rocq, deformalizes it back in en/ru/hi/zh with a structural round trip,
and does so identically in all three roots without memorizing any probe.
R1186-1 through R1186-9 are the issue's R1–R9; R1186-10 is the per-language
probe set its "Probe set and tests" section specifies. The audit and the
fix history live in `docs/case-studies/issue-1186/`.

| R1186-1 | A prompt carrying a formalization command ("formalize", "формализуй", "औपचारिक बनाओ", "形式化") routes to the formalization handler before the search fallback and never yields the canned "Web search requested" paragraph. | Implemented: the `cues` record of `data/seed/formal-targets.lino` declares the command surfaces per language (es too), and `rust/src/solver_dispatch.rs` places `formalization_request` immediately before `product_search` and `web_search`. Pinned by `engine_answers_formalization_request` and `handler_formalizes_hindi_and_chinese_conditionals` in `rust/tests/unit/issue_1186_formalization_task.rs`, and by "the worker answers the cued formalization request" in `rust/tests/web/issue-1186-formalization-parity.test.mjs`. Pinned by `deformalization_round_trip_preserves_structure`, `deformalization_localizes_the_wrapper_prose`, `deformalization_reads_lean_rocq_and_links_notation` and `deformalization_from_lean_answers_in_the_cue_language` in `rust/tests/unit/issue_1186_formalization_task.rs`, and for every target of all 44 probes by `every_rendered_target_deformalizes_with_its_structure_preserved` in `rust/tests/unit/issue_1186_formalization_probe_set.rs`. A target that does not match a seeded template (hand-written Lean with other binder names or tactics) is not read. |
