[Bug][P0] Pending CI aborts unstarted Hive clients and cascades missing receipts: defer and resume before effects

**Type:** Bug · **Priority:** P0

## Reproduction and current behavior

In [Original Hive run 40668396305](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/actions/runs/40668396305) on 2026-10-28, source authentication, build and image preparation passed. Claude delivered and passed [independent verification, job 122773758051](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/actions/runs/40668396305/job/122773758051).

[Agent job 122765110686](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/actions/runs/40668396305/job/122765110686) and [Codex job 122765110642](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/actions/runs/40668396305/job/122765110642) stopped **before client execution**: a required scheduled task-ladder run, `40675356715`, became pending after initial admission. Revalidation correctly refused execution. Downstream verification/persistence then failed because claim/result artifacts did not exist.

The current [hive-delivery-attempt.mjs](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/blob/528053940e8015d16752ee625cffcf15eedc0487/scripts/hive-delivery-attempt.mjs) asserts fresh admission before producing a distinct deferred receipt. This is an orchestration gap; the unstarted clients are not evidence of reasoning failure.

## Outcome that unblocks development

A candidate can wait for real prerequisites and resume safely, with every client outcome represented. Required CI remains required.

## Implementation steps

1. Add explicit pre-effect states: eligible, deferred on named prerequisites, refused, claimed/running, completed, and interrupted/unknown-after-effects.
2. Persist a `deferred-before-effects` receipt before leaving an unclaimed client leg. Include exact source/attempt/client, prerequisite run IDs and statuses, timestamp and proof that no task effects were claimed.
3. Make verification, persistence and aggregation consume this typed outcome rather than trying to download nonexistent claim/result artifacts.
4. Resume or re-admit only when prerequisites for the same immutable candidate settle successfully. Respect effect ownership: never re-execute an already claimed leg just because a sibling was deferred.
5. If a prerequisite fails or the selected source changes, emit refusal or a new attempt identity. Do not mutate old failed receipts into successful ones.
6. Reuse existing claim, custody, protected-reference and cleanup controls. Avoid an unbounded sleep loop occupying a runner; use bounded rechecks and event-driven continuation where possible.
